Church Hub Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 2026

Church Hub is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for all users. We do not tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), sexual exploitation of minors, grooming, or any other content or behavior that places children at risk.

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Church Hub prohibits content or behavior involving the sexual abuse, exploitation, or endangerment of children. Any content or activity that violates applicable laws or platform policies may be removed and may result in restrictions or termination of the responsible account.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

Users must not use Church Hub to create, upload, share, request, distribute, or promote child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation of minors, grooming, or other content that sexually exploits or endangers children.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Anyone who becomes aware of suspected child sexual exploitation, abuse, or other serious child-safety concerns associated with Church Hub should contact us using the contact information provided below.

Reports should include enough information to help us understand the concern, such as the relevant account, content, or other identifying information when safely available.

Review and Action

Child-safety concerns will be reviewed appropriately. Where necessary, we may remove violating content, restrict or terminate accounts, and take other appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and platform requirements.

Contact

For child-safety concerns related to Church Hub, please contact the Church Hub support team through the official contact channel associated with the application.
